# Asset credits

- **Castle Kit 2.0** by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/castle-kit) – CC0 1.0. Walls, towers, siege weapons, flags, trees.
- **Nature Kit** by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit) – CC0 1.0. Trees, flowers, bushes, rocks, fences, mushrooms.
- Troop figures, terrain, sky and effects are generated procedurally in code (`client/src/render`).
- **KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers 1.0** and **Skeletons 1.0** by Kay Lousberg (https://kaylousberg.com) – CC0 1.0. Rigged troop and enemy characters with animations; baked to compact frame data by `tools/bake` into `assets/units/`.
- **KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack** by Kay Lousberg – CC0 1.0. Castle, towers, village buildings, walls, hills, mountains, trees (`assets/hex/`).
- **Impact Sounds** and **RPG Audio** by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds, https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) – CC0 1.0. Hit, coin and click samples in `assets/sfx/`. Horns, arrows, magic and the background music are synthesised in `client/src/audio.ts`.
- **Poly Haven** (https://polyhaven.com) – CC0 1.0. PBR textures in `assets/tex/` (castle_wall_slates, castle_brick_01, leafy_grass, forest_ground_04, dirt_floor, rock_face_03, mossy_rock, brown_planks_05, bark_brown_02, roof_slates_02, snow_02, cobblestone_floor_05), HDRI sky `kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky`, model `boulder_01` (decimated with Blender → `models/ph_boulder.glb`).
